A boat for a few days at sea, with shade when you need it.

The Jeanneau Merry Fisher 795 has a closed wheelhouse, a cabin and a wide swim platform. Small enough for shallow coves, big enough that eight people are not sitting on each other.

On board you don’t have to guess where to go, or when.

Every guest gets a guide that works on the phone, even without signal for what was already opened. This is the real guide, try it.

  • A map of the whole Adriatic with coves, beaches, restaurants, anchorages, marinas and fuel docks. Around Biograd, Pašman and Kornati we picked the places we recommend.
  • Sea routes that go around islands and land, with nautical miles, driving time and a fuel estimate for this boat.
  • Trip planner Pick places and a day, and the guide orders the stops, builds a timetable and gets you back before dark.
  • Wind, gusts and waves on the map and for your route, with a warning when the bora or jugo is stronger than is wise for a small boat.

Before you ask.

Do I need a licence to drive the boat?

Yes. The boat is rented without a skipper only, so whoever drives it needs a Croatian boat licence or a foreign licence recognised in Croatia. Bring it at pick-up.

Is fuel included?

No. You get the boat with a full tank and return it full, or pay for what you used. The guide shows a fuel estimate for every route, so you know the cost roughly in advance.

What if the bora or jugo blows?

We watch the forecast with you. If it is dangerous for a small boat, we move to another day or a shorter route in the lee. Guest safety comes before the plan.

How much are Kornati and Telašćica tickets?

Tickets for Kornati National Park and Telašćica Nature Park are not part of the rental and are paid to the park. The price depends on boat size and where you buy, so check the parks’ official websites before you go.

How much is the deposit and when is it paid?

The security deposit is 1000 € and is paid at the berth before you leave. It is returned after the boat is checked. The rental is paid in two parts: an advance online when you book, the rest on the day.

Can I bring kids and a dog?

Kids are welcome, and we have children’s life jackets on board if you tell us their ages in advance. For pets, write to us in the note.
